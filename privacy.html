Privacy Policy

Miyu · Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version. Miyu works without an account. Your pets, their photos, your daily care history and your reminders stay on your phone. We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

We collect usage statistics — which screens and features are used — to improve the app. They never include your photos, recordings, chats, or anything you type, and you can switch them off in Profile.

The only time anything leaves your phone is when you start an AI check or ask Miyu a question. We tell you before that happens the first time, and you can decline.

1. Who is responsible

Emre Kırca, Türkiye ("we", "us") is the data controller for Miyu. Contact: emreekirca@hotmail.com.

2. No account, no identity

Miyu does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password. When the app first runs it creates an anonymous identifier on our server so your subscription and usage allowance can be recognised. That identifier is not linked to you as a person.

3. What stays on your phone

The following never leaves your device. It is not backed up to us and we cannot read it:

  • Pet profiles: name, species, birthday, breed, weight, gender, your notes
  • Pet photos
  • Daily care tasks and your completion history, streaks
  • Health records: vaccinations, vet visits, treatments and their dates
  • Your conversation history with Miyu
  • Reminders. These are scheduled as local alarms on the device. Miyu has no push notification infrastructure and never sends you anything from a server.
  • Photos and recordings you take for an AI check. Only the most recent 60 checks keep their media; older media is deleted automatically while the written result is kept.

4. What we store on our server

Our backend runs on Supabase (EU – Ireland). We store only:

DataWhy

We never store your photos or recordings on our server. They are passed through for analysis and discarded.

5. What we send to our AI provider

When you start a stool check, a sound check, or ask Miyu a question, we send the following to OpenAI, which performs the analysis on our behalf:

  • The photo or the audio recording for that check
  • Context about the pet: name, species, age, gender, breed, weight, and the notes you wrote
  • Recent check results and upcoming health events for that pet
  • For chat: the messages in that conversation

We deliberately do not send your own name, your birthday, your email, your location, your contacts or your device identifiers.

The notes field is free text. Please do not write information there that you would not want processed by an AI provider.

OpenAI processes this data as our processor and does not use it to train its models. Because OpenAI operates outside the EEA and Türkiye, this involves an international transfer, carried out under the safeguards in OpenAI's data processing agreement.

6. Legal basis

Where the GDPR or KVKK applies: we process data on the basis of performance of a contract (providing the features you asked for) and, for the AI features specifically, your explicit consent, which we request before the first analysis and which you may withdraw at any time by not using those features.

7. How long we keep things

  • On-device data: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • AI check results on our server: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Usage counters: for the current allowance period.
  • Photos and recordings on our server: never stored.
  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to give you a copy of, correct, or delete the data we hold. Because the account is anonymous, deleting the app removes everything held on the device; to delete the records tied to your anonymous identifier, write to emreekirca@hotmail.com from the device in question and we will remove them. Under the GDPR and KVKK you also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

9. Children

Miyu is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Usage statistics. Miyu uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used: which screens are opened, which steps of setup are completed, which features are used and whether a check or a purchase succeeded or failed. These events are linked to the same anonymous identifier described above and to an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, together with general device information (model, operating system version, language, country). They never contain a pet's name, a photo, a recording, a chat message or anything else you type. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected, and the data is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app. You can switch usage statistics off at any time in Profile → Share usage data, and "Delete my data" resets the analytics identifiers on your device.

We use no advertising networks.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
