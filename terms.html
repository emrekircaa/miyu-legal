1. What Miyu is

Miyu is a pet care app. It tracks daily care tasks, stores health records and reminders, and offers optional AI features that look at a photo or a recording and describe what they observe.

2. Not veterinary advice

Miyu provides general information only. It does not diagnose illness, does not prescribe or recommend medication or dosages, and does not replace examination by a licensed veterinarian. No veterinarian-client-patient relationship is created by using this app.

Decisions about your animal's health remain yours and should be made with a veterinarian. Do not delay seeking professional care because of something Miyu showed you.

3. The AI features

The stool check, sound check and chat are powered by an artificial intelligence system. You are interacting with software, not a person and not a professional.

  • AI can be wrong, and can miss things that are present. A photo cannot rule out parasites, infection or internal illness; a recording cannot establish a cause.
  • Miyu is designed never to tell you an animal is healthy. The strongest thing it will say is that nothing concerning is visible in that particular photo or recording.
  • The sound check is experimental and its confidence is deliberately capped.
  • Before your first analysis we ask for your consent to send the photo, recording and pet details to our AI provider. You can decline and keep using the rest of the app.
